# Concept lab

Three concepts, three levels of risk, so the owner always has a safe option and a surprising one:

| Level | What it is | Typical use |
|---|---|---|
| **Safe** | Beautiful, clear product hero. The product and one proof point do the talking. | Regular showcase, busy weeks |
| **Bold** | One strong visual idea or metaphor that stops the scroll, still clearly about the product. | Main post of the week, launches, offers |
| **Wild** | Unexpected, playful or surreal twist. Must still be instantly understood and on-brand. | Experiments (the 20%), occasions, shareable posts |

Each concept is written as:
```
[Safe/Bold/Wild] — [style name]
Idea:     one line
We see:   two lines describing the image
Headline: ≤ 6 words (Roman Urdu/English)
```

## Creative techniques (pick one per concept)

1. **Sensory close-up** — make it tasty: creamy swirl of dahi on a spoon, ghee glistening on a hot roti, condensation drops on a cold milk pouch, steam from a kheer bowl.
2. **Proof shot** — show the claim: spoon standing upright in thick dahi; cup turned upside down and dahi stays; golden grainy texture of real ghee.
3. **Visual metaphor** — "Garmi ka asli ilaaj": dahi bowl as a cool oasis in a hot sunny scene; milk pouch with a sunrise = "subah ka pehla taaza".
4. **Before → after / problem → solution** — dull dry roti → roti with Mooco ghee; plain biryani → biryani with raita.
5. **Desi moment** — sehri table, Jumma lunch, chai time, dastarkhwan, mehman aa gaye, school lunchbox, barish + pakoray.
6. **Scale play** — giant milk pouch towering over a tiny Shorkot street; tiny people climbing a ghee jar (Wild only).
7. **Ingredient story** — the product surrounded by what it becomes: dahi → raita, lassi, kadhi; milk → chai, kheer, sheer khurma.
8. **Wordplay** — Roman Urdu puns and rhymes: "Gaarha itna, dil bhar jaye", "Har ghoont mein taazgi", "Desi ghee, asli khushi".
9. **Contrast** — lime and cream product on a deep Raat Pasture background; cold blue morning vs warm golden ghee.
10. **Seasonal twist** — sunglasses and ice for summer, scarf and steam for winter, lantern for Ramzan, green-white for 14 August.

## Guardrails

- The product stays recognizable and true in every concept; metaphors happen around it, never by changing it.
- One idea per post. If a concept needs two sentences to explain, simplify it.
- No health or cure claims (energy/taste/freshness are fine; "immunity barhata hai" is not).
- Respectful occasions get only a calm Safe concept — offer no Bold/Wild for them.
- Family-friendly, modest, culturally local; avoid anything that could read as mocking, wasteful (e.g. pouring milk away) or unhygienic.
- Wild concepts must still work for someone who sees the post for one second.
