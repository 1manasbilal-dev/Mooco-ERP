# Creative director QA

Run this on the design spec before writing prompts, and again on the generated image in Review & fix. Fix failures yourself; only pass a package that clears every line.

## Checklist

| # | Check | Pass when |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **1-second test** | On a phone, the product and the message are clear in one glance |
| 2 | **One message** | The headline says one thing; everything else supports it |
| 3 | **Thumb-stop** | There is one striking element: texture, color contrast, idea, or scale |
| 4 | **Product truth** | Real pack photo, unchanged; nothing the product doesn't have (fake garnish on the pack, wrong size, invented flavors) |
| 5 | **Brand** | Palette colors, Fredoka/Nunito, real logo placed in Canva, hero promise or a proof point present |
| 6 | **Text** | Headline ≤ 6 words; all on-image text short; contrast rules followed; no text generated by the image model |
| 7 | **CTA** | One clear CTA that matches the current channel (mooco.pk until the app is live) |
| 8 | **Price rule** | Price only on offer posts, and only a real price or `[PRICE]` |
| 9 | **Platform** | 4:5 feed and 9:16 story; story safe zones respected |
| 10 | **Culture & respect** | Family-friendly, modest, right tone for the occasion; no sale or jokes on respectful days |
| 11 | **Claims** | No health/cure claims, no "No.1", no competitor jabs, no khalis claims on other brands |
| 12 | **Feed variety** | Not the same style/product as the last post, unless it is a planned series |

## Generated-image review (step 7)

Look for these common Gemini problems and give an edit line for each:

| Problem | Edit line for the same Gemini chat |
|---|---|
| Pack changed (label text, logo, shape, color) | "Keep the product exactly as in my original photo — same label, logo, text and shape. Only change the background and props." |
| Extra or duplicated packs | "Show only one pack, exactly the one in my photo. Remove the other packs." |
| Text or letters appeared | "Remove all text, letters and numbers from the image. Keep everything else the same." |
| No space for the headline | "Keep the top third of the image clean and empty (soft background only) for a headline. Keep everything else the same." |
| Too dark / too flat | "Make the lighting brighter and softer, like natural morning light, keep the composition." |
| Colors off-brand | "Shift the background colors to soft cream #FFF8E8 with a light lime #98C237 glow, keep the product unchanged." |
| Food looks fake/plastic | "Make the food look real and appetizing: natural texture, soft highlights, slight imperfections, keep everything else." |
| Small text on the pack slightly misspelled (tiny taglines) | "The small text on the pack must read exactly as in my original photo: '[exact text]'. Keep everything else exactly the same." If it still fails and the rest is good, keep the image and, if the text is visible at phone size, place a cut-out of the original pack over it in Canva. |
| Wrong aspect ratio | "Make this a 4:5 portrait image with the same scene, product centered slightly low." |
