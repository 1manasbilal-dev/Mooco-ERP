# Brief builder

A good brief is half the post. It forces one message and one goal, which is what makes a post readable in the one second a thumb gives it.

## Brief template (show it exactly like this)

```
Goal:      [showcase (sells without an offer) / offer (price or deal) / recipe-engagement / occasion greeting / app or website traffic]
Product:   [Mooco product + size, or "none" for greetings]
Message:   [ONE sentence the customer should remember]
Proof:     [one proof point: roz taaza / 100% khalis / farm se seedha / hygienic / taste detail]
Audience:  [families & ghar ki khawateen / young & students / health-conscious]
Platform:  [IG + FB feed 4:5 · Story/Status/TikTok 9:16]
Occasion:  [date or occasion, or "regular"]
CTA:       [mooco.pk par order karein / (app when live) / recipe save karein]
```

## What to infer instead of asking

| Missing | Infer from |
|---|---|
| Product | The photo (match against `products.md` in the brand kit) or the idea text |
| Goal | Words like offer/discount/deal → offer; recipe/banana/kaise → recipe; Eid/14 August → occasion; otherwise showcase |
| Occasion | Today's date, the next 10 days in `occasions-pakistan.md`, and the season table below |
| Audience | Product: milk/dahi/ghee → families; badam doodh/kheer → families + young; offers → everyone |
| Platform | Default: feed 4:5 + story 9:16 version |
| CTA | Brand kit (mooco.pk until the app is live) |
| Proof | The product's strongest truth: milk/dahi → roz taaza; ghee → khalis, desi tareeqa; kheer → ghar jaisa zaiqa |

## Season check (Shorkot, Punjab)

| Months | Season | Feel |
|---|---|---|
| Nov – Feb | Sardi | Warm drinks, steam, ghee, kheer, cozy light |
| Mar | Bahaar / mild | Fresh, bright, green |
| Apr – Sep | Garmi (peak May – Aug; monsoon Jul – Aug) | Cold, refreshing, lassi, dahi, raita |
| Oct | Mild, warm afternoons | Bright sun with cool dairy works; no full-summer clichés |

If the owner asks for a feel that doesn't match the season (e.g. "garmi wali" in December), don't refuse and don't ignore it: translate it into something true today (e.g. "dhoop tez, dahi thanda" in October) and offer the literal version in one line ("Full garmi look chahiye to `full garmi` likh dein").

## When to ask (max 3 questions, one message)

Ask only for facts you cannot invent:
- **Offer posts:** what is the offer (discount, combo, free item), price, and last date?
- **New product or label:** is there a photo of the real pack?
- **Event posts:** exact date/time if it matters (e.g. "Eid ke pre-orders kab tak?").

Never ask about things with a sensible default (colors, fonts, platform, tone).
