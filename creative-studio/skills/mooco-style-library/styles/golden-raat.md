# Golden Raat

**Type:** Core · **Source:** House style (from the brand kit, Raat Glow gradient + ghee label) · **Version:** v1 · **Added:** Oct 2026

## Identity
Andhere mein sunehri chamak — rich, warm and premium, made for desi ghee and night moments.

## When to use / when not
- Use for: Cow and Buffalo Desi Ghee, premium product hero shots, winter warmth (garam doodh, halwa), Ramzan nights, Shab-e-Barat (respectful), Eid eve.
- Avoid for: bright summer freshness (use Doodh Fresh), offers with many details (use Lime Pop), kids-focused posts.

## Palette
- Background: Raat Pasture `#0F1C0A` with a soft Mooco Lime `#98C237` or warm golden glow behind the product (Raat Glow).
- Accents: warm gold/saffron light that echoes the ghee label; Pasture Green `#2F5A1E` layers.
- Text: Mooco Lime or Doodh Cream `#FFF8E8` on the dark background; logo dark version.

## Composition
- Product hero center, 45–60% of the frame, low camera angle for a premium feel.
- Strong rim light outlining the jar/pack; texture close-up (ghee grains, steam) as secondary element.
- Calm dark area at the top for the headline.

## Light & mood
Low-key lighting, warm golden side/back light, deep soft shadows, a little steam or glow. Mood: premium, warm, traditional, rich, calm.

## Props & setting
Dark wood or slate surface, brass or copper bowl of ghee with a spoon, hot roti or paratha with melting ghee, halwa in a brass dish, a few whole spices, soft bokeh lights (Ramzan), a small diya-free warm lamp glow.

## Typography on the post
Headline in Mooco Lime or cream, Fredoka Bold, top; subline cream; CTA pill Mooco Lime with Ink text; logo dark version, small.

## Copy tone
Rich and proud, few words. Examples: "Desi Ghee, Asli Khushi" · "Sardi ki garmahat".

## Gemini prompt ingredients
```
Place the [pack] [position] on a dark wooden surface against a deep green-black background (#0F1C0A). A warm golden rim light outlines the jar and a soft glow rises behind it. Beside it, [a brass bowl of glistening, grainy golden ghee with a spoon / a hot paratha with melting ghee], gentle steam rising. Low camera angle, premium food photography, deep soft shadows, rich warm tones with a subtle lime-green (#98C237) glow in the background. Premium, warm, traditional mood. Keep the top third dark and empty for a headline.
```
Keywords: low-key, golden rim light, glistening, grainy ghee, steam, brass, premium.

## Avoid
Pure black backgrounds, cold blue light, harsh flash, oily/greasy look, clutter, candles or diyas that could read as religious ritual, any party/celebration tone on respectful occasions.
