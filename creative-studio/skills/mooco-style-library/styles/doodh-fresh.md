# Doodh Fresh

**Type:** Core · **Source:** House style (from the brand kit) · **Version:** v1 · **Added:** Oct 2026

## Identity
Subah ki dhoop, cream aur lime ki taazgi — airy, clean, milky and cool.

## When to use / when not
- Use for: Fresh Milk, Dahi, Badam Doodh showcases; morning and summer posts; "roz taaza" messages; recipe covers for cold drinks (lassi, doodh soda).
- Avoid for: text-heavy offers (use Lime Pop), ghee and premium/night posts (use Golden Raat), respectful occasions.

## Palette
- Background: Doodh Cream `#FFF8E8` with a soft Makhan Yellow `#FFF0B3` and Lime Mist `#E8F3CC` glow (the "Doodh Mesh").
- Accents: Mooco Lime `#98C237` through props (mint, cucumber, leaves) and the packs themselves.
- Text: Deep Leaf `#4E7A1E` or Ink `#1A2412` headline on cream; Ink on a lime CTA pill.

## Composition
- Product 50–60% of the frame, slightly below center, standing on a light surface.
- Camera eye-level to 30°, 50 mm feel, shallow depth of field.
- Top third calm and light for the headline; a hero serving (glass of milk, bowl of dahi) beside the pack.

## Light & mood
Soft morning window light from one side, gentle shadows, bright but not overexposed. Mood: fresh, clean, cool, homely, honest.

## Props & setting
Light wood or white marble table, white ceramic or steel glass/bowl, fresh mint, a few ice cubes or condensation drops (cold products), a clay matka in soft focus, muslin cloth, morning sunlight on a kitchen window.

## Typography on the post
Headline top-left or top-center in Deep Leaf; subline in Ink; CTA pill Mooco Lime with Ink text at the bottom; logo small top corner (light version).

## Copy tone
Light, fresh, reassuring. Examples: "Subah ka pehla taaza glass" · "Gaarha. Taaza. Mooco."

## Gemini prompt ingredients
```
Place the [pack] [position] on a light wooden table in a bright, clean Pakistani kitchen. Beside it, [hero serving: a steel glass of fresh milk with a soft splash / a white bowl of thick, creamy dahi with a spoon]. Fresh mint leaves and a small clay matka in soft focus behind. Soft morning window light from the left, gentle shadows. Colors: soft cream background (#FFF8E8) with a light buttery-yellow (#FFF0B3) glow and fresh lime-green (#98C237) accents. Fresh, clean, cool, homely mood. 50 mm lens feel, shallow depth of field. Keep the top third calm and empty for a headline.
```
Keywords: airy, creamy, dewy, morning light, clean, fresh mint, condensation.

## Avoid
Dark or moody lighting, heavy saturation, cluttered props, plastic-looking food, cold blue tints, pure white backgrounds without warmth.
