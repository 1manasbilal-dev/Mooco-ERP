# Lime Pop

**Type:** Core · **Source:** House style (from the brand kit) · **Version:** v1 · **Added:** Oct 2026

## Identity
Mooco ki awaaz buland — bold lime, graphic shapes and one big offer that is impossible to miss.

## When to use / when not
- Use for: offers and deals (11.11, White Friday, combos), new product or label launches, app/website announcements, "order now" pushes, 14 August.
- Avoid for: respectful occasions, slow emotional or recipe posts, premium ghee storytelling.

## Palette
- Background: Lime Pop gradient Mooco Lime `#98C237` → Deep Leaf `#4E7A1E` (diagonal), with Fresh Leaf `#B9D96F` shapes.
- Accent: one Ghee Gold `#F2C14E` badge for the offer.
- Text: Ink `#1A2412` on lime; Doodh Cream `#FFF8E8` only on the Deep Leaf part; never white on lime.

## Composition
- Product cut-out(s) center or right, 40–55% of the frame, with a soft shadow.
- Offer badge (circle or burst) overlapping the product corner, slightly rotated.
- Headline big at the top, deadline line small, CTA pill bottom.
- Usually the **Canva template route**: Gemini makes the background (or a product-on-lime shot), Canva adds the badge and text.

## Light & mood
Bright, punchy, clean studio light, crisp shadows. Mood: energetic, celebratory, confident, urgent (but friendly).

## Props & setting
Abstract rounded shapes, soft lime spheres, milk splashes, a few fresh leaves or ingredients flying (almonds for badam doodh, mint for dahi). No clutter.

## Typography on the post
Headline Fredoka Bold, Ink, very large; badge in Fredoka Bold Ink on Ghee Gold; deadline in Nunito ExtraBold; CTA pill Ink with cream text.

## Copy tone
Short, punchy, playful. Examples: "Double Taazgi, Aadhi Fikr!" · "Sirf Itwaar Tak!"

## Gemini prompt ingredients
```
Place the [pack] [position] on a bold, clean studio background with a diagonal gradient from fresh lime green (#98C237) to deep leaf green (#4E7A1E), with soft rounded abstract shapes in light lime (#B9D96F) and a soft glow behind the product. [Optional: a dynamic milk splash / a few almonds or mint leaves flying around.] Bright, crisp studio light with a soft shadow under the pack. Energetic, celebratory, premium-clean mood. Leave the top quarter and one side empty for a large headline and an offer badge.
```
Keywords: bold gradient, graphic, splash, dynamic, crisp studio light, clean.

## Avoid
More than one accent color, many small texts, neon or cheap-looking effects, cluttered stickers, any price that is not real.
