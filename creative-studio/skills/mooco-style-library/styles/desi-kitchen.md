# Desi Kitchen

**Type:** Core · **Source:** House style (from the brand kit) · **Version:** v1 · **Added:** Oct 2026

## Identity
Ammi ke kitchen ki garmahat — warm, homely Pakistani food moments where Mooco is part of the family table.

## When to use / when not
- Use for: recipes (raita, lassi, kadhi, kheer, halwa, sheer khurma), Shahi Kheer, Jumma lunch, Ramzan sehri/iftar, Eid tables, Mother's Day, family moments.
- Avoid for: big offers with many details (use Lime Pop), minimal premium shots (use Golden Raat).

## Palette
- Natural warm wood, steel, brass and clay tones as the base; Doodh Cream `#FFF8E8` cloth or walls.
- Brand accents: Mooco Lime `#98C237` (mint, chillies, cucumber, the pack), Makhan Yellow `#FFF0B3` light.
- Text: Ink `#1A2412` or Deep Leaf `#4E7A1E` on cream areas; cream text on darker wood only with a soft shadow.

## Composition
- 45° or top-down (flat lay) of a dish being served; the Mooco pack clearly visible beside it, 30–40% of the frame.
- Hands (modest sleeves) serving or stirring are welcome; avoid faces.
- Leave a calm area (cloth, wall or table) for the headline.

## Light & mood
Warm side light like late morning or golden hour through a kitchen window, soft shadows, a little steam for hot dishes. Mood: warm, homely, generous, traditional, inviting.

## Props & setting
Dastarkhwan or wooden table, steel katori and thaal, clay handi, brass bowls, rotis/parathas, fresh coriander and mint, green chillies, cumin, almonds and pistachios for sweets, a simple embroidered cloth.

## Typography on the post
Headline top area on a calm surface (Pasture Green or Ink, check contrast on the real photo); recipe posts may use a small "ingredients" strip; split CTA pill (Ink + cream text + lime chip) at the bottom on a calm area; logo on a white chip if the corner is busy.

## Copy tone
Warm and familiar, like family advice. Examples: "Jumma ka raita, Mooco dahi se" · "Ghar jaisa meetha".

Warm natural wood and brass tones with cream cloth (#FFF8E8) and fresh lime-green (#98C237) accents. Keep a calm empty area at the top for a headline.

Keywords: homely, steam, steel katori, clay handi, dastarkhwan, warm light, generous.

## Avoid
Western/restaurant styling, glossy studio sets, food waste, messy or unhygienic surfaces, faces, alcohol-like glassware, non-local dishes.
