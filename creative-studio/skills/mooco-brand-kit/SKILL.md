# Mooco Brand Kit

Every Mooco output = **chosen style + Mooco identity**. A style (from the style library) decides the look of a post; this kit decides what must stay Mooco in every post. When a style conflicts with this kit, the kit wins on logo, colors of brand elements, product truth and voice.

Visual reference: `assets/brand-board.jpg` (palette, gradients, fonts, color rules, sample light and dark layouts).

| | |

**Audience (in priority order)**
1. Families and ghar ki khawateen — daily doodh, dahi, ghar ka khana; care about purity and trust
2. Young people and students — lassi, shakes, kheer and other treats
3. Health-conscious people — want khalis, fresh, no-milawat dairy

**Proof points (USPs)** — use one per post as the reason to believe the hero promise:
- Roz taaza — made/delivered fresh every day
- 100% khalis — no milawat, powder or chemicals
- Apna farm / direct source — farm se seedha aap tak
- Hygienic process — clean handling and packing

## 2. Colors

Defined from the logo; locked v1.

| Role | Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary | Mooco Lime | `#98C237` | Brand color: shapes, backgrounds, buttons, highlights |
| Green | Fresh Leaf | `#B9D96F` | Light green for gradients and small highlights |
| Green | Olive Leaf | `#6F8A3C` | Logo's dark green ("CO"); icons, large headlines |
| Green | Deep Leaf | `#4E7A1E` | Green **text** on cream (readable) |
| Light | Doodh Cream | `#FFF8E8` | Main light background (use instead of pure white) |
| Light | Makhan Yellow | `#FFF0B3` | Light yellow for gradients and mesh |
| Light | Lime Mist | `#E8F3CC` | Very light green for cards and mesh blobs |
| Accent | Ghee Gold | `#F2C14E` | Offer badges and price tags only — use sparingly |
| Dark | Raat Pasture | `#0F1C0A` | Dark post background (green-black, never pure black) |
| Dark | Pasture Green | `#2F5A1E` | Dark green layers and glows |
| Text | Ink | `#1A2412` | Body text, and any text on lime |
| White | Pure White | `#FFFFFF` | Only behind the logo or in milk shots |

**Color rules (phone readability)**
- ✅ Ink on Lime (contrast 7.7) · ❌ white text on Lime (2.1)
- ✅ Lime on Raat Pasture (8.5) · ❌ Lime text on Cream (2.0) — on light backgrounds use Deep Leaf or Ink for text
- **Measure contrast against what is really behind the element in the finished image**, not against the palette swatch. Photos are warmer and darker than `#FFF8E8`: on a real warm-cream photo background Deep Leaf drops to ~4.3:1 and a lime pill on a wooden table to ~1.2:1. Targets: text ≥ 4.5:1, buttons/pills and badges ≥ 3:1 against the area around them.
- **Headlines on light photos: Pasture Green `#2F5A1E`** (~6.9:1 on warm cream). Deep Leaf only on clean, pale cream graphic backgrounds.
- **CTA on light images: split pill** — Ink `#1A2412` pill with Doodh Cream text ("Abhi Order Karein") + a Mooco Lime chip with Ink text ("mooco.pk →"). A plain lime pill is used **only on dark backgrounds** (Raat Pasture); on cream or wood it disappears.
- **Logo on photos:** if the area behind it is light, warm or busy, place the light logo on a small white rounded chip with a soft shadow (the lime cow is only ~1.5:1 against warm cream).
- Ghee Gold is an accent, not a background; at most one gold element per post
- Dark mode is green-black (Raat Pasture), not navy or pure black — decided by the owner

**Gradients**
| Name | Recipe | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Subah | Makhan Yellow → Fresh Leaf → Mooco Lime, diagonal | Fresh, morning, light posts |
| Doodh Mesh | Doodh Cream base + soft blobs of Makhan Yellow, Lime Mist, Fresh Leaf | Default light background |
| Raat Glow | Raat Pasture base + soft Mooco Lime glow behind the product | Dark / premium / night posts |
| Lime Pop | Mooco Lime → Deep Leaf, diagonal | Sale and offer banners |

In image prompts, describe colors in words **and** hex, e.g. "soft cream background (#FFF8E8) with a gentle buttery-yellow (#FFF0B3) and fresh lime-green (#98C237) mesh glow".

## 3. Fonts

- **Headlines: Fredoka** (Bold/SemiBold) — rounded and friendly, matches the logo letters
- **Body, captions, prices: Nunito** (Regular/SemiBold/ExtraBold)
- Both are free Google Fonts and available in Canva.

## 4. Logo

The logo is a friendly lime cow face where the cow's eyes are the two O's of "MOOCO". Versions:
- **Light backgrounds:** lime cow + dark olive "CO"
- **Dark backgrounds:** lime cow + white "CO"
- With or without the **".Pk"** tag

Rules:
- Use the original logo files from Drive (`Mooco Creative Studio / 03_Brand-Kit / Logo`), preferably transparent PNG. `assets/logo-reference-*.png` are crops for reference only, not for publishing.
- Never let an AI image tool draw, redraw, recolor or "improve" the logo. Leave space and place the real file in Canva.
- Default position: a top corner, with clear space around it of at least the height of one "O".
- Do not stretch, rotate, recolor, outline, or put it on a busy area of the photo.

## 5. Voice

- Language: **Roman Urdu + English mix**. Headline mostly Roman Urdu; tagline stays English.
- Headlines: short — ideally 2–6 words, one idea. Body/caption: 1–3 short lines + a clear CTA.
- Warm and homely, like a trusted neighbourhood dairy: "Roz subah farm se taaza doodh, bina kisi milawat ke."
- Light humour and cow wordplay ("Moo") are allowed occasionally; never mock customers or competitors.
- **CTA right now (app not live):** "mooco.pk par order karein" — works on every phone. Teasers may say "Mooco App jald aa rahi hai!".
- **Main CTA once the app is live = the Android app:** "Mooco App download karein", "App se order karein". Add "iPhone? mooco.pk par order karein" where space allows. WhatsApp only as a backup line (stories, replies).
- Use the official "Get it on Google Play" badge from Google's badge page, unedited — never redraw it. For printed material add a QR code to the app link.
- Delivery wording: local posts say "Shorkot mein ghar tak delivery"; nationwide products say "Poore Pakistan mein delivery". Never promise nationwide delivery for local-only products.

**Examples**
- ✅ "Khalis. Taaza. Mooco." · "Garmi ka Asli Ilaaj!" · "Farm se seedha aap ke ghar"
- ❌ "Pakistan ka No.1 doodh" (unverified claim) · "Sab se sasta" (wrong positioning) · long paragraphs on the image

## 7. Content plan

- **Cadence:** 5 feed posts a week + daily stories (Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp Status, TikTok).
- **Content mix:** Product showcase ~40% · Recipes / use ideas ~30% · Offers / deals ~30%. Occasion posts replace a regular slot that week.
- **App launch (when approved):** 3–5 days of "jald aa rahi hai" teasers → launch-day post with the Play Store badge → a launch offer once confirmed.
- **App growth (after launch):** every post's CTA points to the app. Once a month add an app post ("3 steps mein order", app-only deal).
- **Prices:** show prices **only on offer posts**. Showcase and recipe posts carry the CTA instead of a price.
- **Occasions:** follow `references/occasions-pakistan.md` (live Google Sheet in Drive). Respectful occasions get no sale and no jokes.
- **Tools:** images in **Gemini (Nano Banana, AI Pro plan)**; ChatGPT free as backup. Text, logo and Mooki stickers are added in **Canva Free**, which has no background remover, so use a free remover (Adobe Express, remove.bg) when a cut-out is needed.

## 8. Product truth

- Never let an AI tool generate or redraw a Mooco product, pack, label or price. Always start from the real product photo (`03_Brand-Kit / Product-Photos`) and instruct the tool to keep the product exactly unchanged; only the scene, light and background change.
- Text, prices and the logo are added afterwards in Canva — not rendered by the image model.
- Show products as they really look. No fake textures, sizes or garnish that the product does not have.

## 9. Never say

- Medical or health cure claims (e.g. "immunity barhata hai", "bimari theek karta hai")
- Unverified superlatives ("No.1", "best in Pakistan")
- Anything negative about competitors by name
- Fake or expired offers, or prices not in the product list below
