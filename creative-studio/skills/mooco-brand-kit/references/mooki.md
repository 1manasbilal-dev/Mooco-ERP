# Mooki — Mooco's mascot (ON HOLD)

**Status:** paused by the owner. Do not use Mooki in posts until a master design is approved and saved in Drive (`Mooco Creative Studio / 03_Brand-Kit / Mascot-Mooki`).

## What the owner wants

A cute, joyful baby calf (bachra) that becomes part of the brand identity and can be used in many places (posts, stories, stickers).

## Decisions so far

- **Rejected:** v1 flat sketch (`../assets/mooki-concept.jpg`) — four-legged, all-lime body, no hands, looked flat and alien.
- **Rejected:** Gemini A/B/C exploration — thin toddler body, stiff front pose, pastel lime looked cheap, and the round pink muzzle read as a **piglet**. Never allow a pig-like snout.
- **Liked style reference (owner's):** a white 3D designer-toy bear with chunky green glasses, green dungarees and boots (another artist's character — use only its render quality, never its design or the text/print on its clothes).

What makes that reference work: chunky body with a big head and thick neck, three-quarter pose with attitude, small black eyebrows, rich shaded green (not pastel), small details (buckles, stitching, boot soles, fur tufts), and a cute detail on the head.
