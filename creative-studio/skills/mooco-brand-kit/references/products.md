## Rules

- **Focus:** Mooco's own products are the heroes of nearly all posts. Other-brand items appear only in grocery/delivery posts ("Mooco se poori grocery mangwayein") and never take the lead in a Mooco brand post.
- **Shahi Kheer label is being redesigned.** Until the new pack photo is added, prefer serving shots (kheer in a bowl) and keep the old label out of close-ups. Do not generate or invent a new label.
- **Ghee look:** the ghee jar uses a premium heritage label (deep green + saffron gold), unlike the lime milk/dahi packs. Ghee posts can lean warm, golden and premium (Raat Glow or warm wooden/brass props) while keeping the Mooco logo and palette for text elements.
- **Pack truth:** use these photos as the input image and tell the image tool to keep the pack, label, text and logo exactly unchanged.
- **Khalis/taaza claims apply only to Mooco's own products.** Never attach "khalis", "farm fresh" or other Mooco promises to other brands' items.
- **Other brands:** never edit, recolor or crop their logos misleadingly, never present them as Mooco products, and do not use their brand assets beyond showing the actual pack for sale.
